[TEST] THE LAST SIGNAL

A compact internal story for Completion Card verification.

SESSION 1 — THE STATION

You reach an abandoned communication station after an automated system flags one final transmission. The fence is open. The halls are empty. A damaged transmitter still receives a repeating signal.

First you choose how to meet it: restore power, inspect the dish, or listen without answering.

Then you choose how to work the dying station: decode the buffer, climb the dish, or lock the doors.

Then you must commit: answer the signal, trace its source, or shut the transmitter down.

The session ends. The story does not.

SESSION 2 — BEYOND THE MAPS

The consequence of that commitment arrives immediately. The signal's origin, a last reply, or a stored fragment reconverges at a place the station maps insist does not exist.

First you choose how to meet what your last choice summoned: press toward the origin, wait for the last response, or search the local archive.

Then you choose how to press the consequence: enter the denied room, hold the channel open, or take the fragment.

Then you must commit before the last frequency: follow the origin all the way, keep a copy of what you found, or seal the site.

The session ends. One room remains.

SESSION 3 — THE LAST FREQUENCY

The last room is the unfinished consequence of session two. The remaining message is already here.

First you choose how to enter it: open the remaining channel, read the last log, or stand in the dark.

Then you choose what to do with the remaining words: play them, translate them, or leave them unheard.

Then one final decision remains: preserve the message, destroy it, or leave the frequency running.

The station falls silent. The story ends.
